Team & Workforce Management

TELAT oversees attendance, coaching, real-time queue adherence, and supervisory coverage. Real-time WFM continuously aligns interval staffing against agreed arrival patterns and forecast curves.

Training, Train-the-Trainer (TTT) & QA

Curriculum covers product knowledge, order entry workflows, exception handling, and brand voice. Joint calibration sessions ensure readiness before production launch.
